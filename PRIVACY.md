# Amharic Dictate — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Amharic Dictate is a Windows dictation app made by Endrias F. Abitte. It turns spoken Amharic into
text using a built-in AI speech model (Amharic Speech-to-Text EFA v1) that runs entirely on your
computer.

## What the app does with your information
- **Your voice:** the microphone is used only while you are dictating or recording a voice-profile
  sentence. Audio is processed on your computer and is not sent anywhere.
- **Your text:** the text you dictate is typed into the program you choose and placed on the
  clipboard (marked so Windows keeps it out of clipboard history and cloud sync). It is not sent
  anywhere.
- **Voice profiles:** if you create one, your recordings, corrections and "My words" list are
  stored in your own Windows user folder. You can delete them at any time in Voice profiles.
- **History:** off by default. If you turn it on, a text file of what you dictate is saved in a
  folder you choose on your computer.

## Network use
Dictation never uses the internet. The version downloaded from GitHub checks once a day for a newer
version by asking GitHub for the latest release number; nothing about you or your dictation is
sent, and you can turn this off in Settings. The Microsoft Store version doesn't make this check:
the Store handles updates.

## What we don't do
No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no data sold or shared. The app has no
servers: we never receive your audio, text or personal information.
