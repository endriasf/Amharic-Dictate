Free, offline Amharic dictation for Windows, powered by the Amharic Speech-to-Text EFA v1 AI speech model. Speak Amharic and your words appear as text in Word, Outlook, Teams, your browser or any other program.

Free and open source.

  • AI speech recognition: the built-in AI model Amharic Speech-to-Text EFA v1 turns your speech into Amharic text. It's included in the installer.
  • Works offline: the AI runs on your computer. Nothing you say is sent anywhere.
  • Easy corrections: click any word to fix, remove or replace it.
  • Learns your voice: optional voice profile for better accuracy.

Dictate

  1. Click where you want the text to go (for example a Word document).
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space. A box appears and the icon turns red.
  3. Speak Amharic normally. Your words appear in the box as you speak.
  4. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space again (or click ✓ Done). The text is typed into your document.

The text also stays on the clipboard, so you can paste it again with Ctrl + V.

Live dictation into Microsoft Word

When you start dictating while Word is the active window, your words go straight into the document as you speak, at the cursor:

  • Corrections you make in the dictation box (replace, remove, insert, join, punctuation, Undo) update the document immediately.
  • You can also edit the dictated text directly in Word; the dictation box updates to match.
  • Typing anywhere else in the document is never touched.
  • Word's cursor follows your words, as if you were typing. Click somewhere else in the document (or type there) and keep talking: the next words go at the new spot, and the box starts fresh for that passage. Clicking inside the passage you are dictating keeps it as one.
  • ✓ Done simply finishes (the text is already there). ✕ Cancel removes the dictated text.

Turn it off in Settings → General → Live dictation into Microsoft Word. In other programs, the text is pasted when you finish.

Buttons in the dictation box

Button What it does
↶ Undo Undo the last change (up to 14 steps)
⏸ Pause Stop listening for a moment, for example to talk to someone. Click again to resume

⧉ Copy Copy the text without closing the box
። ፣ ፤ ? ! « » ( ) Insert punctuation
✕ Cancel Close the box and throw the text away

You can change the shortcuts, or switch to push-to-talk (hold a key while speaking), in Settings.

Voice commands

Say these words and the app inserts the mark instead of the words:

Say You get
አራት ነጥብ ።
ነጠላ ሰረዝ ፣
ድርብ ሰረዝ ፤
የጥያቄ ምልክት ?
ቃለ አጋኖ !
ቅንፍ ክፈት … ቅንፍ ዝጋ ( … )
ጥቅስ ክፈት … ጥቅስ ዝጋ « … »
ትእምርተ ጥቅስ « the first time, » the next time
አዲስ መስመር New line
አዲስ አንቀጽ New paragraph
ያንን ሰርዝ Delete the last word

Numbers: spoken numbers become digits. For example ሃያ አራት → 24, ሁለት መቶ ሀምሳ → 250. A single small number such as "አንድ ሰው" stays as words.

Fix mistakes

In the dictation box, while you are still dictating:

  • Click a word: it is highlighted. Then either say the correct word, click one of the suggestions, or use the buttons: 🗑 Remove, ⇤ Join previous, Join next ⇥, ➕ Insert before / after, ★ My words.
  • Click the space between two words: say the words you want to insert there, or click Join words to remove the space.
  • Click a punctuation mark: remove it, or change it to another mark.

Colours in the box:

  • Red underline: probably misspelled. Click the word to see corrections.
  • Orange: the app wasn't sure it heard this word correctly.

Train it to your voice, add your own words, learn from corrections

All three are in one window: tray icon → Voice profiles…. Everything stays on your computer.

Create your voice profile

The first time you start the app, it asks "Who's dictating?". Type your name to create your profile. To add another person, open Voice profiles… and click New… next to Who's dictating. Each person's training, words and corrections are kept separately.

Train the app to your voice

Step 1: record the setup reading (tab 1. Setup reading)

  1. A sentence is shown. Press ● Record and read it aloud at your normal speed.
  2. Press ■ Stop when you finish.
  3. Press ▶ Play back to check it. If you stumbled, press ● Re-record.
  4. Press ✓ Save & next for the next sentence, or Skip sentence to skip one.
  5. Aim for 50 sentences (5–10 minutes). Training needs at least 30. Reading more, up to 300, gives better accuracy. You can close the window and continue another day.

The level bar shows your microphone volume. The window warns you if a recording is too quiet or too loud.

Step 2: train (tab 2. Train profile)

  1. Click Start training. It runs in the background, usually for 15–40 minutes. You can keep working, and even keep dictating.
  2. When it finishes, the app compares your voice profile with no profile on recordings it did not train on. If your profile is more accurate, it switches on automatically and the window shows the before and after results.
  3. To turn your profile off, untick Use this voice profile when dictating.

Read more sentences later and click Start training again whenever you like. Each retraining uses all your recordings.

Add your own words (My words)

For names, places, organisations and terms the app gets wrong or underlines in red. There are two ways to add them:

  • While dictating: click the word in the dictation box, then click ★ My words.
  • In the window: open tab 3. My words, type the word (or several words), then press Add or Enter.

To remove words, select them in the list and click Remove selected.

Words you add are preferred when the app is choosing between similar words, near-misses are corrected to them automatically, and they are never marked as misspelled. The list belongs to the profile, so each person has their own.

Learn from your corrections

  1. Open tab 4. Learning and tick Learn from my corrections.
  2. Dictate as usual. When you fix a word in the dictation box and then press ✓ Done, the app saves that sentence's audio with your corrected text. - Dictations you cancel are never saved. - Sentences that are too unclear to match safely, such as sentences you rewrote completely, are skipped.
  3. With Retrain automatically after 25 new corrections ticked, your profile improves itself in the background when you are not dictating. Otherwise, click Start training in tab 2 whenever you want.

The tab shows how many corrections are saved. Delete saved corrections removes them; your setup reading is kept. Untick Learn from my corrections before dictating anything you don't want stored on the computer.

Move a profile to another computer

Click Export… to save the profile as a .zip file. On the other computer, open Voice profiles… and click Import….

Settings

Tray icon → Settings:

  • Hotkeys and push-to-talk
  • How text is inserted: when you finish (default), after each sentence, or to the clipboard only
  • Spelling check and automatic spelling fixes
  • Speech model and performance options
  • Start with Windows

Tips and troubleshooting

Problem Try this
Nothing appears when I speak Check the microphone in Windows Settings → System → Sound → Input. Speak a little closer to the microphone
Words are wrong Speak at a steady pace, use a headset, do the voice-profile setup, add names to My words
Background voices get typed Use ⏸ Pause or push-to-talk mode
Text doesn't appear in a program Programs running as Administrator block typing from normal apps. Use ⧉ Copy and paste with Ctrl + V
Amharic letters show as boxes Use an Amharic-capable font such as Ebrima or Nyala (built into Windows)

Privacy

Everything you dictate is processed on your computer by the built-in AI model. Nothing is uploaded, and no online AI service is used.

Updates: once a day the app asks GitHub for the version number of the newest release, so it can tell you when an update is available (right-click the tray icon → Download update…). Nothing about you or your dictation is sent, and nothing is downloaded or installed automatically. Turn this off in Settings, or check by hand with Check for updates in the tray menu. To update, run the new installer: your settings and voice profiles are kept.

Like any AI, the speech model can mishear words, especially names, rare words and noisy audio. Check the text before you send or print it. Voice training and My words reduce mistakes. Dictation history is off by default. Voice-profile recordings and corrections stay in your Windows user folder (%APPDATA%\AmharicDictate) and can be deleted in Voice profiles.
